자주 묻는 질문

게임에서 오류가 발생해요.

게임을 완전히 종료한 뒤 다시 실행해 보시고, 앱스토어 또는 구글 플레이에서 최신 버전인지 확인해 주세요. 같은 문제가 계속되면 위 정보를 담아 이메일로 알려 주세요.

결제 및 환불은 어떻게 하나요?

결제와 환불은 각 스토어(Apple App Store, Google Play)의 정책에 따라 처리됩니다. 환불은 해당 스토어의 구매 내역 페이지를 통해 요청해 주세요. 결제 후 상품이 지급되지 않았다면 구매 영수증(주문 번호)과 함께 이메일로 문의해 주세요.

계정 및 데이터를 삭제하고 싶어요.

개인정보 열람·정정을 요청하고 싶어요.

위 이메일로 요청해 주세요. 자세한 내용은 개인정보 처리방침을 참고해 주세요.

Support

Thank you for playing games by Retry Studio. If you have a question, want to report a bug, or have a privacy-related request, please contact us at the email address below.

Contact email

Supported Games

  • Guardian Survival
  • Block Crusher: Action Puzzle

Please Include in Your Message

Sending the following information helps us assist you faster.

  • Game name
  • Your device model and OS version (e.g., iPhone 15 / iOS 18, Galaxy S24 / Android 15)
  • Game version
  • A description of the problem (when it happened and on which screen)
  • A screenshot or video, if possible

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm experiencing a bug in the game.

Please fully close and relaunch the game, and check that you have the latest version from the App Store or Google Play. If the problem continues, email us with the information listed above.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled according to each store's policies (Apple App Store and Google Play). Please request a refund through the purchase history page of the relevant store. If you did not receive an item after paying, email us with your receipt (order number).

I'd like to delete my account and data.

You can request deletion directly on each game's account deletion page below.

  • Guardian Survival: Account deletion page
  • Block Crusher: Action Puzzle: Account deletion page

If you have trouble using the page, email us at the address above.

I'd like to request access to or correction of my personal information.

Please send your request to the email address above. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

I have feedback or a suggestion.

We welcome your feedback at any time. Send it by email and we will take it into account in development.
